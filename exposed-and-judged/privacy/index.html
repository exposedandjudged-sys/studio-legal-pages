Privacy Policy

Exposed And Judged Studio
Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Exposed And Judged Studio handles information when connecting to third-party social media services, including TikTok.

About Exposed And Judged Studio

Exposed And Judged Studio is a privately operated content-production and publishing application used to create, review, prepare, and publish original fictional storytelling content for the Exposed And Judged social media channels.

Information accessed from connected platforms

When an authorized account owner connects a TikTok account, the application may access information permitted by the scopes authorized through TikTok, including basic account identity information such as an account identifier, display name, username, and profile information.

If publishing functionality is authorized, the application may also use permissions necessary to prepare or submit media to the authorized TikTok account and obtain information needed to determine the status of those submissions.

How information is used

Information received from TikTok is used only to provide requested account-connection and content-publishing functionality, verify the identity of the connected account, determine available publishing capabilities, and report the status of authorized publishing activity.

TikTok information is not used for advertising, profiling, or the sale of personal information.

Authentication and credentials

Connections to TikTok use TikTok's official authorization mechanisms. The application does not request or store the user's TikTok password.

Authentication credentials and authorization tokens used by the local Studio application are stored using secure operating-system credential storage where supported and are not intentionally exposed in exported content packages or public application pages.

Content publishing

Exposed And Judged Studio does not publish content to a connected platform merely because an account has been connected. Publishing actions require the application's publishing workflow and an authorized connected account.

Sharing of information

Information obtained through TikTok is not sold or rented to third parties. Information may be transmitted to TikTok when necessary to perform an authorized TikTok API operation.

Data retention

The application retains only information reasonably necessary to maintain authorized account connections, publishing records, and operational history. Authorization credentials may be removed when the connected account is disconnected or access is revoked.

Revoking access

An account owner may disconnect TikTok from Exposed And Judged Studio. Access may also be revoked through TikTok's account and application authorization controls.

Third-party services

Use of TikTok is also governed by TikTok's own terms, policies, and privacy practices. Exposed And Judged Studio does not control TikTok's independent handling of information.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality, platform integrations, or applicable requirements change. The current revision date will be displayed on this page.

Contact

Questions concerning this Privacy Policy may be directed to the operator of Exposed And Judged Studio through the contact information provided on the associated Studio website.
