Exposed And Judged Studio

Exposed And Judged Studio is a privately operated content-production and publishing application for the Exposed And Judged social media channels.

What the application does

Exposed And Judged Studio supports the creation, review, preparation, and publishing of original fictional storytelling videos.

The application provides tools for managing scripts, narration, captions, visual planning, video preparation, publishing metadata, and approved final media.

Social media integration

Exposed And Judged Studio may connect to authorized social media accounts, including TikTok, using official platform APIs.

Connections are initiated and authorized by the account owner. Platform access is used only to provide the requested account connection and publishing functionality.

Authorization can be disconnected or revoked by the account owner.
