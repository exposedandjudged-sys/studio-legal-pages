Exposed & Judged creates fictional, short-form storytelling videos built around difficult choices, unexpected turns, and the question everyone wants to answer: what would you do?

01 / The Studio

A private production workflow, from premise to approved final video.

Exposed & Judged Studio is a privately operated content-production application used to create, review, prepare, and publish original fictional storytelling videos for Exposed & Judged social media channels.

Its tools support scripts, narration, captions, visual planning, video preparation, publishing metadata, and review of final media.

02 / Publishing

Owner-authorized connections. Human-reviewed publishing.

The Studio may connect to authorized social media accounts, including TikTok, through official platform authorization and APIs. Connections are initiated by the account owner and can be disconnected or revoked.

Connecting an account does not publish content. Content and publishing selections are reviewed through the Studio workflow before any supported publishing action is requested.
