Exposed And Judged Studio

Exposed And Judged Studio is a private content-production application used to create, review, prepare, and publish original fictional storytelling videos for the Exposed And Judged social media channels.

The application may connect to authorized social media accounts using official platform APIs. Account authorization is initiated by the account owner and can be revoked.
